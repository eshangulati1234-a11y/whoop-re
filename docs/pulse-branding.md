Symbol uses approximately 55 percent of square width and 32 percent height, substantial negative space. Flat monochrome, immaculate vector-like edges. No letters, no P loop, no text, no blue or other color, no gradient, no glow, no shadow, no texture, no border, no outer rounded corners, no device mockup, no tiny details. Only the black square and white centered pulse symbol. 1024x1024.
